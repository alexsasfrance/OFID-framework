OFID – Ontological Framework of Imbricated Degrees

Conceptual foundation of the OFID framework

The Ontological Framework of Imbricated Degrees (OFID) introduces a conceptual perspective in which physical descriptions are limited by degrees of inaccessibility, while global dynamics remains unitary.

Overview

OFID proposes that physical descriptions are fundamentally constrained by information accessibility. In this perspective, limits on local description do not imply a breakdown of global unitarity.

The framework is intended to clarify conceptual aspects of quantum decoherence, gravitational effects, black holes, and the relation between local accessibility and global dynamics.

It is not presented as a new dynamical law, but as a conceptual framework for interpreting certain regimes in which local reconstructibility may become limited.
