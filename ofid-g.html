OFID-G – Testable formalization

Operational extension of the OFID framework

OFID-G introduces a testable operational perspective derived from the OFID conceptual framework, with emphasis on experimentally accessible regimes and possible limits of quantum coherence reconstructibility.

Overview

OFID-G extends the OFID framework toward experimentally testable regimes by introducing an operational perspective on quantum coherence reconstructibility.

It explores the possibility that, even under globally unitary dynamics and controlled environmental conditions, limitations may arise at the level of local reconstructibility rather than dynamical evolution.

In this perspective, the relevant question is not whether coherence is dynamically destroyed, but whether it remains fully reconstructible once all known decoherence channels have been independently characterized and controlled.

If a residual limitation correlated with gravitational configuration were observed, it would not indicate a breakdown of quantum theory, but rather suggest the existence of structural constraints on accessibility that are not reducible to standard decoherence.

A possible observation of such a limitation would therefore not signal a failure of quantum mechanics or of decoherence theory, but rather indicate that the standard framework may be incomplete at the level of accessibility, even if it remains fully valid at the dynamical level.
