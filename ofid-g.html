OFID-G – Testable formalization

Operational extension of the OFID framework

OFID-G introduces a testable operational perspective derived from the OFID conceptual framework, with emphasis on experimentally accessible regimes and possible limits of quantum coherence reconstructibility.

Overview

This formulation extends the conceptual framework toward experimentally testable regimes. It focuses on an operational perspective in which one may probe whether reconstructibility of quantum coherence encounters a genuine limit beyond standard environmental decoherence.

The OFID-G perspective is intended to connect the conceptual structure of OFID with experimental inquiry in mesoscopic systems and gravitationally relevant settings.
